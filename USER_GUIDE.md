# Call Track — User & Administrator Guide

> **Version: Patch 1.1**  
> This comprehensive guide covers both daily operational workflows for **Freelancers / Callers** and administrative management for **Admins**.

---

## ⚡ Initial Setup & Getting Started

### 1. Setup Options

#### Option A: Local Node.js Setup
1. Open a terminal in the `call-track` directory.
2. Run database migration and seeding:
   ```bash
   npm run db:push
   npm run db:seed
   ```
3. Build and start the application:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   npm run start
   ```
4. Access the app at `http://localhost:3000` (or `https://calltrack.flexibook.ai` if configured with Cloudflare Tunnel).

#### Option B: Docker Desktop Setup (Recommended for Windows)
1. Install and start [Docker Desktop](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop).
2. Right-click **`setup.ps1`** → **Run with PowerShell**.
3. Use the desktop shortcuts generated by setup:
   - **Start Call Track**: Launches container and opens the browser.
   - **Stop Call Track**: Safely shuts down the container.

---

### 2. Seed Login Credentials (`SEED_CREDENTIALS.txt`)

During initial seeding (`npm run db:seed`), cryptographically secure passwords are auto-generated and saved to **`SEED_CREDENTIALS.txt`** located in the `call-track` root folder:

```text
============================================
 GENERATED SEED CREDENTIALS (save these!)
 Admin:      admin@calltrack.local       →  <Generated_Password>
 Freelancer: freelancer@calltrack.local  →  <Generated_Password>
============================================
```

> ⚠️ **Important**: Open `SEED_CREDENTIALS.txt` to retrieve your initial passwords. Store them securely and update them after initial login.

---

### 3. Password Security & Rate Limiting (Patch 1.1)

To ensure account security and protect client contact data:

- **Password Policy**: All new accounts (created via Registration, Add Freelancer, or Add Admin) and password resets must adhere to:
  - Minimum **8 characters**
  - At least **one uppercase letter** (`A–Z`)
  - At least **one numeric digit** (`0–9`)
  - At least **one special character** (`! @ # $ % &` etc.)
  - Common weak passwords (e.g. `admin123`, `password1`) are blocked.
- **Brute Force Protection**: In-memory sliding window rate limiters automatically throttle repeated failed authentication and registration attempts.

---

### 4. Installing as a Mobile App (PWA)

Call Track is optimized for mobile outreach:
1. Open the app URL in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iOS).
2. Tap browser options / Share button → **"Add to Home Screen"**.
3. Launch Call Track directly from your home screen for full-screen dialing.

# 📞 Freelancer & Caller Guide

*As a Freelancer / Caller, you manage assigned leads, dial prospects, capture structured call outcomes, and schedule follow-ups.*

---

## Logging In
1. Navigate to the app URL.
2. Select **Freelancer** on the role switch.
3. Enter your email and password from `SEED_CREDENTIALS.txt` (or your approved registration account).
4. Tap **Sign In**.

---

## Freelancer Dashboard — 4 Tabs

### Tab 1: My Leads (Queue)
Your primary active outreach pipeline.

- **Urgency Summary Strip**:
  - 🔴 **Red (Urgent / Critical)**: Assigned > 72 hours ago without outreach.
  - 🟡 **Orange (Pending)**: Assigned 24–72 hours ago.
  - 🟢 **Green (Fresh)**: Assigned within the last 24 hours.
  - 🔵 **Attempted**: A call link has been initiated for this lead.
- **Sort Toggle**:
  - Tap **"Urgent First"** to sort your queue by urgency (oldest assigned SLA risks first).
  - Tap **"Priority Sort"** to sort by lead tier (A vs B priority).
- **Next Lead in Queue**: The top card highlights the highest priority lead. Tap **Start Call Session** to dial immediately.
- **Contact Cards**:
  - Displays prospect name, phone, company, and real-time **Urgency Badge**.
  - **Topic to Discuss (Blue Box)**: Critical notes and context provided by Admin. Always review before dialing!
  - Green **Call** button to launch the Call & Feedback interface.

---

### Tab 2: Today's Calls
Displays scheduled follow-up calls due today.
- Shows scheduled time and contact details.
- Tap **Call** to start the call session.

---

### Tab 3: Follow-ups
Tracks all upcoming and overdue follow-up commitments.
- Items tagged with 🔴 **Overdue** are past their scheduled date and require immediate attention.
- Tap **Call** to reconnect and record next steps.

---

### Tab 4: Activity Log (New in Patch 1.1)
Review and manage your complete historical call log.

- **Call History List**: Chronological timeline of all logged calls and interactions.
- **Outcome Badges**: Visual indicators for Connected, Not Interested, Converted, Callback, etc.
- **Editing Past Call Records**:
  1. Locate the call entry in the **Activity Log** tab.
  2. Tap the **Edit (Pencil)** button on the right side of the card.
  3. The **Edit Activity Log Modal** opens.
  4. Update the **Outcome**, **Notes**, **Response**, **Interest Level**, or adjust the **Next Scheduled Follow-up**.
  5. Tap **Save Changes** to instantly update records and sync with Admin reports.

---

## Call & Feedback Workflow

1. **Initiate Call**: Tap the green **"Call [phone number]"** button. Your phone dialer opens automatically.
2. **Select Outcome**:
   - **Connected**: Spoke with the decision-maker or representative.
   - **No Answer**: Phone rang with no response.
   - **Busy**: Line was engaged.
   - **Wrong Number**: Incorrect or invalid contact info.
   - **Not Interested**: Prospect declined product/service.
   - **Callback Requested**: Prospect requested a call at another time.
3. **Feedback & Interest Level** (if Connected):
   - Select **Hot 🔥**, **Warm ☀️**, or **Cold 🧊**.
   - Enter specific notes regarding prospect requirements and objections.
4. **Schedule Next Action**:
   - Select next activity type (**Call**, **Email**, **Meeting**).
   - Set follow-up date and time.
5. **Save**: Tap **Save Call Record**.

---

## In-App Notifications (🔔)
- The bell icon in the header alerts you to new contact assignments and admin reminders for overdue follow-ups.
- Tap a notification to mark it as read.

# 🛠️ Admin & Management Guide

*Admins oversee lead ingestion, assign queues, monitor SLA compliance via the Urgency Meter, review call outcomes, and manage team users.*

---

## Admin Dashboard Overview

### Top KPI Summary Strip
- **Total Contacts**: Total database prospect count.
- **Calls Today**: Cumulative calls logged by all callers today.
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of contacts marked converted.
- **Overdue Follow-ups**: Total pending activities past their due date across the team.

---

## Admin Navigation & Main Views

### 1. Analytics & Reports View
Select **Analytics** from the top navigation. Explore 4 specialized sub-tabs:

#### Sub-Tab A: Overview & Urgency Meter
- **Lead Outreach Urgency Meter**: Real-time SLA tracker for assigned leads awaiting first contact:
  - 🔴 **Critical (>72h)**: Immediate SLA breach risk.
  - 🟡 **Pending (24–72h)**: In danger of exceeding SLA.
  - 🟢 **Fresh (<24h)**: Recently assigned.
  - 🔵 **Attempted**: Outreach initiated.
- **Drill-Down Filtering**: Click any urgency metric card to automatically filter contacts by that urgency level.

#### Sub-Tab B: Team Performance
- View **Freelancer Workload Table**: assigned leads, calls logged, connected call rate, and verified call percentages per caller.

#### Sub-Tab C: Sales Pipeline
- Visual breakdown of the sales funnel, tag coverage, and follow-up pipeline.

#### Sub-Tab D: Call Outcomes Report (New in Patch 1.1)
- Comprehensive log of every call attempt across the entire organization.
- **Filters**:
  - **Date Range**: Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or All Time.
  - **Freelancer / Caller**: Filter by specific caller or view All Team members.
- **Data Table**: Shows Date/Time, Prospect Name & Phone, Caller Name, Connected Status, Outcome Badge, and Notes.

---

### 2. Contacts Management View
Select **Contacts** from navigation.

- **Search & Filters**: Search by name, phone, company, status, priority (Tier A/B), and tags.
- **Add Contact**:
  1. Click **Add Contact**.
  2. Enter Name, Phone, Email, Company, Priority, and optional Discussion Topic.
  3. Save contact.
- **CSV Bulk Import**:
  1. Ensure your CSV has headers: `name,phone,email,company,source` (plus optional `tag`, `call_priority`).
  2. Click **CSV Import** and select your file.
- **Assigning Leads**:
  1. Click **Assign** on any unassigned or queued lead.
  2. Select the Freelancer from the dropdown.
  3. Enter the **Topic to discuss** (e.g., specific pitch points or promo details).
  4. Submit. The caller is notified immediately.
- **Contact Details & Audit Trail**:
  - Click on any contact card to view full profile details, call history logs, and assigned SLA urgency.

---

### 3. Overdue Follow-ups View
Select **Overdue** from navigation.

- Displays all follow-ups that have passed their due date across all callers.
- **Send In-App Reminders**: Click the orange **Remind (Bell)** button on any overdue item to send an instant push notification to the assigned freelancer.

---

### 4. Freelancers & User Management View
Select **Freelancers** from navigation.

- **Manage Applications**: Approve, reject, or suspend freelancer accounts.
- **Add Freelancer**: Directly create and provision approved freelancer accounts.
- **Add Admin (New in Patch 1.1)**:
  1. Click the **Add Admin** button.
  2. Provide Name, Email, and a strong Password adhering to the password policy.
  3. Creates an administrative account with full management privileges.
- **Password Resets**: Trigger secure password resets for team members.
- **Bulk Reassign**: When offboarding or rebalancing workloads, reassign all contacts from one caller to another in a single click.

---

## 📋 Recommended Admin Daily Workflow

```text
Morning:
  1. Check KPI Strip and Lead Outreach Urgency Meter on Analytics tab.
  2. Address Critical (>72h) leads: reassign if callers are bottlenecked.
  3. Go to Overdue tab → Send reminders for overdue follow-ups.
  4. Import or add new leads and assign with clear Discussion Topics.

During the Day:
  5. Monitor Calls Today progress on KPI Strip.
  6. Review incoming calls via Analytics → Call Outcomes Report.

End of Day / Week:
  7. Check team conversion rates and caller connected call metrics.
  8. Review pipeline health and prepare queues for the next shift.
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Where do I find the initial login passwords?**  
A: Passwords are automatically generated during database setup and saved in `SEED_CREDENTIALS.txt` in the app directory.

**Q: Can a caller edit their notes or outcome if they made a mistake?**  
A: Yes. Callers can switch to the **Activity Log** tab (Tab 4) and tap the **Edit (Pencil)** icon on any call record to update the outcome, notes, or follow-up schedule.

**Q: How do I create another Admin user?**  
A: Log in as an Admin, navigate to the **Freelancers / Team** view, and click the **Add Admin** button. Ensure the password meets the minimum 8-character complexity rules.

**Q: What do the Urgency Meter colors mean?**  
A: Red = Critical (>72 hours since assignment without outreach), Orange = Pending (24–72 hours), Green = Fresh (<24 hours), Attempted = Outreach was initiated.
